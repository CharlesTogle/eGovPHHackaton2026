# NDRRMC Official RDANA (Rapid Damage Assessment and Needs Analysis) Post-Disaster Reference

---

## 1. Executive Purpose & Operational Mandate

The **Rapid Damage Assessment and Needs Analysis (RDANA)** is the official post-disaster assessment tool institutionalized by the Office of Civil Defense (OCD) and National Disaster Risk Reduction and Management Council (NDRRMC).

Conducted immediately within **0 to 72 hours post-disaster**, RDANA provides an authoritative "snapshot" of disaster impacts to determine:
1. The extent of human casualties and displaced populations.
2. The operational status of critical lifelines (power, water, roads, communications).
3. Immediate humanitarian needs across standard response clusters.
4. Local Government Unit (LGU) response capacity and required national augmentation.

---

## 2. Official OCD RDANA Form-1 Structure

Field assessment teams (Barangay DRRMC, Municipal/City DRRMO, and OCD Deployment Teams) collect post-disaster telemetry using the standard **5-Section RDANA Form-1**:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    OFFICIAL OCD RDANA FORM-1 SECTIONS                   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ SECTION I: General Disaster Profile & Geographic Location               │
│ SECTION II: Human Impact & Displaced Population (Inside/Outside ECs)    │
│ SECTION III: Lifelines, Infrastructure & Critical Facilities Status     │
│ SECTION IV: Sectoral Damage & Urgent Humanitarian Needs Clusters       │
│ SECTION V: Local Response Capacity & Recommended Augmentation Requests  │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

### SECTION I: General Disaster Profile
* **Event Type:** Typhoon, Heavy Rainfall/Flood, Earthquake, Volcanic Eruption, Landslide, Fire.
* **Date & Time of Occurrence / Landfall:** Timestamp of primary impact.
* **Geographic Coverage:** Target Region, Province, City/Municipality, Barangays, Sitios/Puroks.
* **Emergency Operations Center (EOC) Status:** Red Alert / Blue Alert / White Alert.

---

### SECTION II: Human Impact & Displaced Population
* **Total Affected Population:** Total affected families and individuals.
* **Displacement Status:**
  * **Inside Evacuation Centers (ECs):** Count of active ECs, registered families, and individuals inside.
  * **Outside Evacuation Centers:** Displaced families staying with host families or informal shelters.
* **Casualties Breakdown:**
  * **Dead:** Confirmed vs Unconfirmed headcounts.
  * **Missing:** Reported missing persons.
  * **Injured:** Hospitalized vs Outpatient.
* **Vulnerable Sectors Headcount:** Infants (0-5 yrs), pregnant/lactating mothers, senior citizens (60+), and Persons with Disability (PWD).

---


### SECTION III: Sectoral Damage & Urgent Needs Clusters

Post-disaster relief needs are organized into **5 Official NDRRMC Response Clusters**:

1. **Food & Non-Food Items (NFI) Cluster:**
   * **Family Food Packs (FFPs):** Calculated at `1 FFP per family for 3 days`.
   * **Ready-to-Eat Food (RTEF):** Required for immediate 0-24 hour distribution.
   * **Hygiene Kits:** Soap, sanitary napkins, toothbrushes, towels.
   * **Sleeping Kits:** Sleeping mats, blankets, mosquito nets.
2. **WASH (Water, Sanitation, Hygiene) Cluster:**
   * **Potable Water:** Required liters/day (`Minimum standard: 15 L/person/day`).
   * **Water Purification:** Need for water treatment tablets or mobile filtration units.
   * **Sanitation:** Portalets or temporary toilet ratio (`Standard: 1 toilet per 20-50 evacuees`).
3. **Health & Psychosocial (MHPSS) Cluster:**
   * **Medical Supplies:** First aid kits, tetanus toxoids, maintenance medicines.
   * **Mental Health:** Psychosocial support teams for traumatized evacuees.
4. **Shelter Cluster (Housing Damage):**
   * **Partially Damaged:** Repairable roof/wall damage; structure remains habitable post-repair.
   * **Totally Damaged:** Completely destroyed, washed away, or collapsed.
   * **Shelter Materials Needed:** Tarpaulins, corrugated sheets, timber, nails.
5. **Protection Cluster:**
   * Safe spaces for women/children, solar lighting in ECs, security patrols.

---

### SECTION IV: Local Response Capacity & Augmentation Requests

* **Local Relief Inventories:** Remaining food packs and medical kits in LGU warehouses.
* **Official Request to Regional OCD / NDRRMC:** Specific items, logistics, or rescue teams requested for immediate national augmentation within 24-48 hours.

## 3. Data Collection Workflow & Official Cascading

```
[ Field Assessment Team / Barangay Tanods / Resident] ──► (Completes RDANA Form-1 Field Survey) 
                     │
                     ▼
[ Barangay DRRM Committee (BDRRMC) / Field Assessment Team ] ───────► (Validates Sitio/Purok Telemetry)
                     │
                     ▼
[ City / Municipal DRRMO ] ──────────────────► (Synthesizes LGU RDANA & DROMIC Form 1)
